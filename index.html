Software courses軟體課程

Eight introductory courses and one debug clinic. Each course is one live session of about 4 hours with one worksheet. Together they give you a map of the whole robot software stack: where each piece sits, what it assumes, and how it breaks.

八堂入門課，加一場除錯工作坊。每堂課是一次約 4 小時的現場課程，配一份學習單。全部上完，你會有一張整個機器人軟體架構的地圖：每一塊在哪裡、它假設了什麼、它會怎麼壞掉。

How the worksheets work. The numbered Parts are what we do together in the session. Boxes and Parts marked Go deeper hold ideas that take longer to sink in, for after the session. Step-by-step walkthroughs open from the worksheet; use ← → and press E for the explanation. Every page has a 中文 button, or press L.

學習單怎麼用。有編號的部分是課堂上一起做的。標成延伸的方塊和部分，放的是比較需要時間消化的觀念，課後再看。逐步解說從學習單開啟，用 ← → 前後移動，按 E 看說明。每一頁都有 English 按鈕可以切換語言，也可以按 L。
