Software courses

Eight introductory courses and one debug clinic. Each course is one live session of about 4 hours with one worksheet. Together they give you a map of the whole robot software stack: where each piece sits, what it assumes, and how it breaks.

How the worksheets work. The numbered Parts are what we do together in the session. Boxes and Parts marked Go deeper hold ideas that take longer to sink in, for after the session. Step-by-step walkthroughs open from the worksheet; use ← → and press E for the explanation.
